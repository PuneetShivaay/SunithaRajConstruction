## Home `/`
1. Hero: headline, subline, CTAs (Get a Quote / WhatsApp), image crossfade
2. Stats strip: years, projects delivered, service lines, machines
3. Client logo strip
4. Services: 4 cards linking to `/services/[slug]`
5. Featured projects: 6 items linking to `/projects`
6. Process timeline: Design, Concept, Implementation, Installation, Testing, Maintenance
7. Why us: 6 value cards (Expertise, Quality, Modern methods, Sustainability, Adaptability, Supplier network)
8. Testimonials
9. FAQ (short)
10. CTA band (gold)
