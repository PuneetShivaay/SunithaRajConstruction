# 01: Project Overview

## Business
- **Company:** Sunitharaj Construction, Gomti Nagar, Lucknow (Uttar Pradesh)
- **Services:** Civil construction, Interiors, Electrical, Waterproofing
- **Clients:** Government and private projects (residential, commercial, roads, STP/WTP)
- **Primary conversion:** Quote enquiry via WhatsApp or the contact form

## Goals
1. Present a **professional, premium** brand that matches the gold and steel logo.
2. Generate **more qualified leads** (WhatsApp, calls, form).
3. Rank for **local search** ("construction company Lucknow", "waterproofing Lucknow", etc.).
4. Make projects and services **easy to update** later.

## Problems with the current site
- Editorial serif styling feels "café" rather than construction or architecture.
- Low-contrast cream palette clashes with the gold logo.
- Single page means weak SEO, and the site is set to `noindex`.
- Lists look like spreadsheets, image quality is inconsistent, and trust signals are weak.
- The copy-protection script hurts UX and accessibility. **It will be removed.**

## Target audience
- Homeowners and builders in Lucknow and nearby areas
- Commercial clients (shops, malls, offices)
- Government departments and contractors

## Success criteria
- Lighthouse: Performance ≥ 90, Accessibility ≥ 95, SEO = 100
- LCP < 2.5s on 4G mobile
- Every page has a clear CTA (WhatsApp / Call / Quote)
- Indexed by Google with LocalBusiness structured data

## Out of scope (for now)
- Logo redesign (planned later)
- Client portal, payments, blog (possible later phases)
